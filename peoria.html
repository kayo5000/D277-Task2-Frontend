Life along the Illinois River

Peoria

Follow the Illinois River to Peoria. Its riverfront and surrounding neighborhoods offer a city experience shaped by the water.

Five things to know

Population
113,150 residents (April 1, 2020 Census).
Incorporated as a city
1845
Region
Central Illinois, on the Illinois River
Classification
Urban city. This guide uses an urban description based on its city setting and density of 2,359.0 people per square mile (2020).
Average income comparison
$38,466 per person; $7,940 below the Illinois figure of $46,406. Both values use 2020–2024 ACS estimates, in 2024 dollars.

A few places to start

  1. Start at the downtown riverfront.
  2. Explore the Peoria Riverfront Museum.
  3. Take in the views along Grandview Drive.
