The lakefront metropolis

Chicago

A city of neighborhoods, a vast lakefront, and a skyline that rewards looking up. Chicago offers a different view of Illinois around every corner.

Five things to know

Population
2,746,388 residents as of the April 1, 2020 Census (U.S. Census Bureau, n.d.-a).
Incorporated as a city
1837 (City of Chicago, Office of the City Clerk, n.d.).
Region
Northeastern Illinois, on Lake Michigan
Classification
Urban city. This guide uses an urban description based on its city setting and density of 12,059.8 people per square mile in 2020 (U.S. Census Bureau, n.d.-a).
Average income (per person) comparison
$50,086 per person; $3,680 above the Illinois figure of $46,406. Both values use 2020–2024 ACS estimates, in 2024 dollars (U.S. Census Bureau, n.d.-a, n.d.-b).

A few places to start

  1. Walk along the Lake Michigan waterfront.
  2. Explore the public spaces of Millennium Park.
  3. Discover the architecture along the Chicago River.
