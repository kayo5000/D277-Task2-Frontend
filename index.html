Discover Illinois.

From the state capital to the lakefront and the river, get to know three distinct Illinois cities.

A place to begin

Illinois is a Midwestern state with Springfield as its capital. This guide connects the history of Springfield, the scale of Chicago, and the river setting of Peoria. Choose a city to explore its people, location, and story.
