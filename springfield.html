The state capital

Springfield

Meet Illinois at its political and historic heart. Springfield brings state government and the story of Abraham Lincoln into the same walkable city.

Five things to know

Population
114,394 residents (April 1, 2020 Census).
Incorporated as a city
1840
Region
Central Illinois
Classification
Urban city. This guide uses an urban description based on its city setting and density of 1,871.1 people per square mile (2020).
Average income comparison
$42,670 per person; $3,736 below the Illinois figure of $46,406. Both values use 2020–2024 ACS estimates, in 2024 dollars.

A few places to start

  1. Explore the Illinois State Capitol area.
  2. Visit the Lincoln Home National Historic Site.
  3. Learn about the city at Lincoln Library.
