One state. Three perspectives.

Discover Illinois.

From the state capital to the lakefront and the river, get to know three distinct Illinois cities.

A place to begin

Illinois is a Midwestern state with Springfield as its capital (State of Illinois, n.d.). This guide connects the history of Springfield, the scale of Chicago, and the river setting of Peoria. Choose a city to explore its people, location, and story.

Population figures come from the April 1, 2020 Census. Income figures come from the 2020–2024 American Community Survey, in 2024 dollars. Sources for population and income: Chicago (U.S. Census Bureau, n.d.-a), Peoria (U.S. Census Bureau, n.d.-b), and Springfield (U.S. Census Bureau, n.d.-c). Sources for incorporation dates: Chicago (City of Chicago, Office of the City Clerk, n.d.), Peoria (City of Peoria, n.d.), and Springfield (Lincoln Library, n.d.). Per-capita income is total income divided by population, not a wage or median household income.
